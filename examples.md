> Hey folks! So this PR basically kicks off the long-overdue effort to leverage the new retry framework under the hood of the upload pipeline. Initially we went with a naive approach of retrying ad infinitum, but after some back-and-forth in review it transpired that this could effectively DDoS our own S3 gateway, hence the pivot to a capped exponential backoff (base 500ms, ceiling 30s, max 5 attempts). We also took the opportunity to sunset the legacy UploadHelper.retryLoop() since it's no longer pulling its weight. Long story short, uploads should now be way more resilient, though I'd be remiss not to mention that the flaky UploadIT test still occasionally rears its ugly head on CI (see #1287). > This PR moves upload retries to the new retry framework and limits how many times an upload is retried. An upload that fails once can still succeed on a later attempt, so fewer uploads should end with an error.
>
> ## Retry policy
>
> - A failed upload gets at most 5 attempts.
> - The wait between attempts starts at 500 ms and grows after each failed attempt. This is called exponential backoff.
> - The wait never goes above 30 s.
> - [clarify: which errors make the pipeline retry an upload]
>
> ## Why retries have a limit
>
> If there is no limit, every failed upload keeps sending requests to our S3 gateway. When many uploads fail at the same time, these requests could overload the gateway. The growing wait and the 5-attempt limit put a cap on how many requests each failed upload sends.
